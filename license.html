Sale terms

Company license

These are the terms to put on the order before a property management company pays. Have a lawyer review them before the first paid invoice.

Pricing · Reviewer

  1. The license is for the named company and the properties listed on the order. A manager seat is for an employee or agent of that company.
  2. The company may not resell, sublicense, or give the reviewer to another management company.
  3. The reviewer is a specialist aid. It is not a determination by a state allocating agency, HUD, USDA, or the IRS. The company remains responsible for the tenant file and for any finding on a review.
  4. Income and rent limits, forms, and verification windows must be confirmed for the county, the effective date, and the monitoring agency.
  5. The fee is earned for access during the paid term. Annual orders are paid in advance. A property added midyear is billed for the remaining months.
  6. Either party may end the license at the end of the paid term. Ending it does not erase a correction letter already delivered.
  7. Tenant files uploaded for review stay with the company. Do not use a resident file in a sales demo.
