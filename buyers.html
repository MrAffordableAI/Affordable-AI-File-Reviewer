Bolt-on or standalone

Three ways to use the desk

A large company can bolt it onto Yardi. A small company can use it with no property system. A housing authority can use it on the files it monitors. Same reviewer.

Open the reviewer · Pricing · Yardi bolt-on

Bolt-on

For a company already on Yardi Voyager, Breeze, RealPage, AppFolio, MRI, ResMan, or Entrata. Paste the certification row, drop the file, send the correction letter back to the site. The ledger stays in that system.

Independent, smaller companies

For an owner or manager with a few Tax Credit, HOME, RD, HUD, or Section 202 properties and no Yardi. The manager drops the file, picks the state and the programs, and prints the correction. No interface and no setup.

Site desk is $199 a property each month. One property is enough to start.

Housing authorities

An authority uses the same desk on properties it owns or monitors that are Tax Credit, HOME, RD, HUD multifamily, or Section 202. The authority names the properties on the order. Site staff get the correction letter. The authority keeps the monitoring file.

Public housing and voucher files that live on Form 50058 are a different packet. This desk reviews the 50059, TIC, RD 3560-8, and HOME file. Do not sell it as a 50058 or PIC replacement.

What stays the same

  • All 50 state allocating agencies.
  • Manager correction letter and stacking order.
  • Tax Credit findings tagged to Form 8823 lines 11a, 11b, 11f, 11g, and 11i.
  • The company or authority remains responsible for the file. This is not an agency determination.
