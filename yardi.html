Bolt-on, not a replacement

The file desk next to Yardi

Yardi Voyager and Breeze keep the ledger, the waitlist, and the certification record. This desk takes the file those screens point at and writes the manager correction. Same pattern for RealPage, AppFolio, MRI, ResMan, and Entrata.

What to tell a Yardi shop

  • Do not replace Voyager. Paste the certification row, drop the tenant file, and send the correction letter back to the site.
  • Tax Credit findings are tagged to Form 8823 lines 11a, 11b, 11f, 11g, and 11i, so the compliance lead sees report risk, not only a missing document.
  • All 50 allocating agencies are named. Idaho uses the IHFA stacking order. Other states use that agency's manual where it is stricter than the federal floor.
  • The letter tells the manager the correction and how the file should be stacked.

Against the tools already in the market

  • EliseAI Affordable LeasingAI qualifies prospects, quotes total monthly leasing price in a few states, and watches fair-housing phrasing. It is a leasing assistant. It is not the dropped-file correction desk. EliseAI also staffs analysts to review files. This product is the desk the manager uses, with the letter and the stacking order.
  • Yardi Affordable and RentCafe store the certification. They do not hand the site manager a correction letter from the scanned file.
  • Full affordable suites such as ExactEstate price the system of record at about $5 a unit a month. This is the file layer on top of the system they already pay for.

Do not claim a live Voyager interface. The bolt-on today is the certification paste plus the file drop. A direct interface is a later company contract, not a promise on this page.
